# Example Training Plan

User-facing plan. Keep this focused on instructions, targets, and upcoming schedule adjustments. Do not store private coach reasoning or retrospective analysis here.

## Nutrition & Fueling

*   **Base Days:** Eat at maintenance and keep protein consistent.
*   **Protein:** 1.6-2.2 g/kg/day.
*   **Long Sessions:** Practice 30-60 g carbohydrate/hour after 2 hours.

## Pacing, Surfaces & Rest Rules

*   **Easy Endurance:** Conversational effort, mostly Zone 1-2.
*   **Surfaces:** Gradually build trail and hill exposure.
*   **Rest Days:** Keep rest days real; easy walking or mobility only.

## Strength Maintenance

Two short full-body sessions per week unless endurance load or injury risk requires reducing to one.
